BUVIJAG AI — FULL-FEATURE PRESENTATION DATASET

Purpose:
This is a synthetic retail dataset intentionally containing both normal business data
and controlled data-quality problems so the BUVIJAG presentation can demonstrate
data health, missing values, duplicate keys, anomalies, inventory intelligence,
AI insights, charts, action plans, simulations and reporting.

SIZE
250 rows × 20 columns

INTENTIONAL DATA-QUALITY SCENARIOS
1. Missing values across Customer_State, Payment_Method, Supplier, Review_Score,
   Delivery_Days, Product, Current_Stock, Reorder_Level, Customer_ID, Category,
   Unit_Price, Revenue, Quantity and Order_Date.
2. Duplicate Order_ID values to demonstrate duplicate-key detection.
3. One exact duplicate row to demonstrate duplicate-row detection.
4. Inconsistent text values such as "upi", "Tamilnadu", "late", and "electronics ".
5. Suspicious numeric values including zero price, negative revenue and negative quantity.
6. Inventory problems including low stock, zero stock and negative stock.
7. Sales/revenue outliers.
8. Delivery-delay outliers and lower review scores.
9. Repeated Customer_ID values to demonstrate repeat-customer analysis.
