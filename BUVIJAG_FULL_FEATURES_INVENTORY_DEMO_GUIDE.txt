BUVIJAG FULL-FEATURE INVENTORY DEMO DATASET

250 rows × 21 columns.

IMPORTANT INVENTORY SCENARIOS
• Rows 1–25: Critical/Reorder — Current_Stock is below Reorder_Level.
• Rows 26–60: Low Stock — Current_Stock is below Reorder_Level but above the critical range.
• Rows 61–65: Out of Stock — Current_Stock = 0.
• Rows 66–68: Negative stock — intentional data-quality issue.
• Remaining rows: Stock OK — Current_Stock is above Reorder_Level.
• Daily_Sales is included so inventory analysis can demonstrate sales velocity/days of stock where supported.

DATA HEALTH SCENARIOS
• Missing values in important fields.
• Duplicate Order_ID values.
• One exact duplicate row.
• Inconsistent text values.
• Zero price.
• Negative revenue.
• Negative quantity.
• Inconsistent date format.
